Not a café.
A gathering.

The Sunday Bunch is a home-based themed brunch concept built around front-yard/home dining, rotating menus and a deliberately intimate reservation experience.

In 2026, public reporting describes the concept as relocated and reopened in Ang Mo Kio. The address published in a May 2026 report is Blk 601 Ang Mo Kio Avenue 5, Singapore 560601.

The experience is designed around the edition: a theme, a table, a menu and a small group of guests.
