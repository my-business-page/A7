# Website Blueprint — THE SUNDAY BUNCH

## Research snapshot — 20 Sep 2026
- Home-based themed brunch/cafe concept.
- 2026 reporting says it relocated and reopened in Ang Mo Kio; a May 2026 report gives Blk 601 Ang Mo Kio Avenue 5, Singapore 560601.
- Instagram: @the_sunday_bunch.
- Reservations are tied to Instagram and limited booking releases; older coverage describes Google Forms and monthly rotating menus.
- Concept: front-yard/home dining, changing themed menus, coffee, matcha and food; no walk-ins for the reservation experience.
- Phone/WhatsApp: not independently verified.
- Standalone official website: no verified standalone official domain found in this pass.
Sources: https://vibes.8world.com/food/home-cafe-the-sunday-bunch-3149411 ; https://eatbook.sg/the-sunday-bunch/

## Site map
Home / Current Edition / Menu / Reservations / Story / Gallery / Visit / FAQ / Contact.

## Core UX
The homepage behaves like an “edition launch”: current theme, date, remaining booking status, hero dish, Reserve button. Previous editions become an archive.

## Visual
Editorial brunch magazine: warm ivory, botanical green, ink black, food-photography-led layouts. Use subtle vinyl/record and garden references without clutter.

## Motion/VFX
- Opening “new edition” reveal.
- Menu cards rise/fade with stagger.
- Food image parallax and masked crop transitions.
- Reservation slots animate like editorial tickets.
- Background foliage uses extremely subtle depth/parallax.
- Scroll exits fade/scale; all reveals reverse upward.
- Optional WebGL grain/film-dust layer, disabled on low-power devices.

## Functional
Edition CMS-like JSON data; reservation CTA to current official booking flow; limited-slot status; menu archive; allergy note; map/address disclosure policy; Instagram integration; mobile-first accessibility.

## Accuracy
Current menu, dates, opening schedule and exact pickup/dining address must be owner-confirmed before launch. Do not rely on 2023 menu prices.
